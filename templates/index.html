About

I'm a 4th-year engineering student at INSA Toulouse (Informatique et Réseaux), specializing in SIEC — real-time and safety-critical embedded systems. That's the responsible half of me.

The other half just likes to take things apart. I'm drawn to operating systems (microkernels especially), experimental systems, new interfaces, and the tools that make complex systems legible instead of intimidating.

Mostly I care about building things that are actually useful — whether that's a kernel design in my head or an end-to-end tool that shaves hours off a tedious workflow.

Focus Areas

  • Operating systems & microkernels
  • Experimental systems development
  • Developer tools & systems visualization
  • Human–system interaction & new interfaces
