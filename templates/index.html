About

I'm a 4th-year student at INSA Toulouse studying engineering in Informatique et Réseaux, specializing in SIEC (Systèmes Informatiques Embarqués Critiques) — real-time and safety-critical embedded systems.

On the research & development side, I'm drawn to operating systems (microkernels especially), experimental systems, new interfaces, and the tools that shape how we build and visualize systems.

I care about concepts, tangible visualizations, and making complex systems legible through great developer tools.

Focus Areas

  • Operating systems & microkernels
  • Experimental systems development
  • Developer tools & systems visualization
  • Human–system interaction & new interfaces
